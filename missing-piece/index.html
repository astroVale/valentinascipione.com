Don't take this lost feeling into January

A short check to help you work out what needs to change in work and life, and choose where to start before the year ends.

About 15 minutes. Nothing to write, nothing is saved.

Step 1 · Picture next year

Take one minute before you start

Imagine it's a Monday morning one year from now, in December 2027. You wake up, and you're actually looking forward to the week ahead. What's different? What are you doing, who's around you, how do you feel? Keep that picture in mind. We'll come back to it.

Step 2 · Notice where you are today

Now come back to today

Which of these feel true for you right now? Select every statement that sounds like you, and go with your first reaction.

Select at least one statement first.

More than one thing came up for you. Which one made you nod?

Step 3 · Reflect

Five questions to sit with

Take a minute with each one. There's no need to write anything down.

  1. When does this feeling show up most in my week, and what does it make me want to do?
  2. If this came back, what would it give me that really matters to me?
  3. What does my mind keep telling me about why now isn't the right time?
  4. What small discomfort would I be willing to feel to move towards it, like an awkward conversation or an evening without catching up on work?
  5. What's the smallest step I could take before the year ends?

Step 4 · Your letter

A letter from future You

Remember the picture from the start? This letter is from the person in it.

Dear you,

It all started with something small, in December 2026, when I…

    With love,
    You, one year from now

    Step 5 · Make it real

    Put your step in your calendar

    Pick the step that feels right in your letter, choose a day before the year ends, and add it to your calendar. Small is perfect. This is where January starts.
