Do these thoughts sound familiar?

  • "Is polyamory really for me?"
  • "I want this, so why does it hurt so much when I imagine my partner on a date with someone else?"
  • "What if my partner falls in love with someone else, and I become less important?"
  • "Am I really interested in this, or am I saying yes because I'm scared of losing them?"
  • "Everyone else seems so comfortable with it. Why am I struggling?"

These are some of the most common questions at the start. I asked most of them myself.

My story

I was 35 when I discovered polyamory. I remember my walk to work every morning, with two voices arguing in my head. One said: "What am I doing? Maybe I should go back to how things were, before it's too late." The other said: "Going back is a mistake. You weren't happy before, and this is what you want."

I read every book I could find, joined the discussion groups of the polyamory community and learned a lot from a good friend who became my mentor. Step by step, the two voices learned to listen to each other.

Today I'm a certified relationship coach, and I help people through that same beginning, with the support I wish I'd had.

What we can work on together

  • Finding out if polyamory is right for you, and what you want from it
  • Talking with your partner about opening your relationship, honestly and without shame
  • Dealing with jealousy when it shows up, instead of reacting to it
  • Feeling secure in yourself when your partner meets someone new
  • Making time for more than one relationship, plus friends, work and yourself
  • Shaping each relationship in a way that works for the people in it

What I've learned along the way

  • Jealousy is a feeling, and it will show up. You can learn to sit with it and listen to what it tells you.
  • Your feelings belong to you. That can be hard to accept at first, and it also means you have more power over them than you think.
  • Every good relationship needs honest communication and consent, whatever its shape.
  • Love can be unlimited. Time is not.
  • The first relationship you build is the one with yourself.

How I work

I help you find your own answers. Through questions and reflection, we look at what you really want, explore your options and choose steps that feel right for you, at your own pace.

I work with individuals, online, in English or Italian.

Coaching is for people who feel ready to look forward. If you are going through a crisis, a therapist is the best first step.
